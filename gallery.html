A day at DramaWize

Twelve moments. One transformation.

Every child walks in a little unsure. Ninety minutes later they have warmed up, built a story from scraps, turned a scarf into a hundred characters, rehearsed with a partner and stood tall in front of the room.

  1. 1Day one. Watching, quiet.
  2. 2Shy hands, hidden face.
  3. 3Warm-up: bodies first.
  4. 4Story cards spark ideas.
  5. 5A poem in pieces, rebuilt.
  6. 6One scarf, 100 characters.
  7. 7Wand, bowl, partner: a scene.
  8. 8Ideas land on paper.
  9. 9A shape no one makes alone.
  10. 10Showtime: their own scene.
  11. 11Standing tall, filling the room.
  12. 12One voice, heard by everyone.

Small moments, big skills.

Confidence, communication, collaboration and critical thinking — built through play, not lectures.

A note on privacy

No child's face is shown anywhere on this site or our social channels without a parent's written consent. Photos are chosen or covered to protect identity — the stars you see are deliberate. If you'd like your child featured (or never featured), just tell Manasi.
