The Premise

Not acting classes. Life classes.

The skills life will eventually grade your child on are the skills schools were never designed to teach.

Schools teach children to write. They teach them to calculate. They do not, by design, teach them to speak with presence, hold a room, manage their nerves, regulate their emotions or recover from a stumble in real time.

Yet these are the competencies their next chapter will quietly demand — in classrooms, on stages, in interviews, in friendships, in front of themselves.

The oldest classroom in the world is the stage. Children learn 90% more when they physically engage — and drama, taught well, is the only discipline that builds voice, presence, empathy, courage and collaboration in a single weekly session.
